Our Philosophy

Most investors buy and hold and hope. We believe hope is not a strategy. Markets trend, they reverse, they punish complacency. A fund that only publishes its wins is not a fund. It is marketing. We publish everything — the alpha and the drawdowns, the theses and the mistakes. Complexity should be earned.
