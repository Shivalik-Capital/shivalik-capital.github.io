# Shivalik Capital

🌐 https://shivalik-capital.github.io/

**Independent quantitative investment research, systematic strategy development, and macroeconomic analysis.**

Shivalik Capital is a personal research project focused on exploring data-driven investing through quantitative finance, market research, and systematic trading strategies.

The goal is simple: publish transparent research, explain investment ideas clearly, and document the process behind building systematic investment models.

- Performance analysis

- Strategy assumptions and limitations

## Philosophy

Markets reward discipline over prediction.

Rather than relying on opinions or short-term speculation, Shivalik Capital focuses on:

- Evidence-based investing
- Systematic decision making
- Long-term thinking
- Transparent research

Every strategy published includes its methodology, assumptions, and limitations.
