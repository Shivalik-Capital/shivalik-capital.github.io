Why Shivalik Capital Exists

To Our Investors,

Shivalik Capital was founded in June 2026 in Panchkula, Haryana right at the foothills of the Shivaliks with a single guiding belief: that disciplined and rigorously structured investing can outperform the instinct-driven decisions of the average market participant.

This is our inception letter. We are beginning this journey from nothing but only a systematic framework, a rigorous backtesting engine, and a commitment to publishing everything we find, even our failures with honour.

Why Shivalik Capital Exists

Most retail investors in India approach the market the same way. They buy an index or a multi cap fund, hold it forever, and live in the delusion of becoming the next "crorepati". This is neither a methodical strategy nor does it have a roadmap for the ever-changing market dynamics of our country. It is an assumption — that markets always go up, that time in the market always beats timing the market, and that no analytical edge is possible for an individual investor.

Well, We respectfully disagree.

The Nifty 50 has declined significantly over the past year, while global markets continue to rise despite fears of a bubble, and has fallen from approximately 26,500 points to averaging around 23,500 points — a stark fall that has silently crumbled millions of portfolios of passive investors who were never told to ask: is there a better way?

Shivalik Capital exists to answer that question systematically, stringently, and honestly — publishing our methods, our results, and our mistakes transparently.

Strategy: Moving Average Crossover on Nifty 50

Our first strategy is completely straightforward. We believe in keeping things simple, if a fund cannot explain its strategy clearly, it shouldn't be managing your money.

The thesis: Market trends. When short-term momentum turns positive relative to longer-term momentum, it signals a shift in market character worth participating in. When it turns negative, capital preservation takes priority over chasing returns.

The mechanics: We calculate a 20-day and 50-day simple moving average on the Nifty 50 daily closing price. When the 20-day average crosses above the 50-day average, we enter a long position. When it crosses or stays below, we exit and preserve cash. No leverage. No shorting. No guesswork.

The backtest period covers December 2025 through June 2026 — six months that proved to be one of the more difficult stretches the Nifty 50 has seen in recent years.

Results

Approach Return (6 Months) Notes
Buy & Hold Nifty 50 -10.10% Passive index exposure
Shivalik MA Strategy -3.80% 20/50 day crossover
Alpha Generated +6.30% Outperformance vs index

During one of the most difficult six-month periods the Nifty 50 has faced in recent years, our strategy preserved capital significantly better than a submissive approach of holding funds — losing 3.80% against the index's 10.10% decline.

The reason is straightforward: the strategy spent most of this period in cash. With the 20-day average consistently below the 50-day average throughout the prolonged downtrend, the model correctly identified that conditions did not justify market exposure. It did not predict the crash. It refused to submit to erratic and volatile Global dynamics over the past months.

What We Are Cautious About

Six months of data is not sufficient to declare any strategy effective. This result may reflect the strategy's genuine merit — or it may reflect the fact that a trend-following approach happened to suit a trending market. We do not yet know.

In the upcoming quarters we will test this strategy across longer time horizons, run new models across different market conditions and additional indices such as the NASDAQ and S&P 500, and compare the results of each model. We will publish those results honestly — including when the strategy underperforms, examining everything from our successes to where we went wrong.

A fund that only publishes its wins is not a fund. It is marketing tool.

What Comes Next

In Q3 2026 we will extend our backtest to a multi-year window, introduce additional strategy variants, and begin tracking forward performance in real time. We will create blogs and a Youtube Channel to better explain our methodolgies and varying approaches to Global Markets as we move forward. We will also publish our backtesting methodology in full so that any reader can independently verify our results.

We are building this in public, from the beginning, with nothing to begin with and nothing to hide.
